## Equations and maths
- magnification = image size ÷ real size (students recall this; it is not in the given list for biology)
- rate = 1 ÷ time (or change ÷ time); percentage change = (final − initial) ÷ initial × 100
- energy (J) = mass of water (g) × 4.2 × temperature rise (°C) (HT, calorimetry)
