## Equations
- BMI = mass (kg) ÷ (height (m))² — given in the statement 7.17 itself ("using the BMI equation"). Students should still learn it.
- waist:hip ratio = waist circumference ÷ hip circumference (same units).
- Thresholds used: BMI categories (NHS: <18.5 underweight, 18.5–24.9 healthy, 25–29.9 overweight, 30–39.9 obese, ≥40 severely obese). Waist:hip "higher risk" above 1.0 (men) and 0.85 (women) — as given in Save My Exams' Edexcel diabetes note; other sources quote 0.90 (men). The deck states the threshold in each question.
