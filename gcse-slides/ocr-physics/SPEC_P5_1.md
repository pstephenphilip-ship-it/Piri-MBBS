# SPEC_P5_1: OCR J260 Combined Science B (Twenty First Century), P5.1 What is radioactivity?

Chapter P5 Radioactive materials. In J260 (Combined Science B) the chapter has **two** sub-topics:
- **P5.1 What is radioactivity?** (this deck, Part 1 of 2)
- **P5.2 How can radioactive materials be used safely?** (next deck, Part 2 of 2)
- P5.3 How can radioactive materials be used to provide energy? (fission and fusion) is in **Physics B J259 only**, not J260.

The PMT "OCR (B)" P5.1 and P5.2 topic-question sets use the same titles.

## Equations
- There are no P5.1 equations on the J260 equation sheet, and none to recall. Half-life work uses repeated halving, (½)ⁿ and ratios (maths skills).

## Practical
- No PAG is linked to P5.1 (UNVERIFIED). The deck uses a **suggested practical**: modelling random decay and half-life with 120 dice. It covers method, apparatus drawn, variables, safety, results, a graph drawn to scale and sources of error. The model half-life is ln 2 / ln 1.2 = 3.80 throws.
