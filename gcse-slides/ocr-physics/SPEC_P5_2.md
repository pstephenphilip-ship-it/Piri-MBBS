## Practical
- No PAG is named for P5.2 (UNVERIFIED). Slides 8–9 are a **suggested practical**: a teacher demonstration that identifies the radiation from an unknown source using paper, 5 mm aluminium, 25 mm lead and a GM tube, with background correction. They cover method, apparatus drawn, variables, safety (CLEAPSS L93: under-16s do not handle sources), results (3 repeats, means, corrected values) drawn to scale, conclusion and sources of error.
