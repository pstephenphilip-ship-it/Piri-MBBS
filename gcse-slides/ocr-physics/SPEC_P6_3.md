# SPEC_P6_3: OCR J260 Combined Science B (Twenty First Century), P6.3 How does the particle model relate to material under stress?

Chapter P6 **Matter – models and explanations**. This deck is Part 3 of 3 (P6.1, P6.2, P6.3) in J260. P6.4 (pressure in fluids) and P6.5 (Big Bang) are Physics B J259 only (see SPEC_P6_1.md).

**J260 physics has chapters P1–P6 only. There is no P7.** OCR examiners' reports describe J260/03 (Foundation) and J260/07 (Higher) as testing chapters P1–P6. **P6.3 therefore completes J260 physics.**

## What was verified from search extracts
- **The sub-topic title.** "P6.3 How does the particle model relate to material under stress?" Sources: the tutordex J259 topic listing, PMT "Topic 6.3 Stress – OCR (B) Physics GCSE" flashcards, and the OCR J259 Advance Information 2022.
- **The topic content.** It covers:
  - stretching, bending or compressing needs more than one force (this is the first statement)
  - elastic vs plastic deformation, using the particle model
  - force–extension for springs and other simple systems
  - linear vs non-linear relationships
  - the spring constant
  - work done / energy stored in a stretched spring
  - a PAG 2 practical
  
  Source: search summaries of the tutordex J259 P6.3 page, whose text was cut off after statement 1.
- **The particle-model background.** "Compressing or stretching the material changes the separation of the particles, and the forces between the particles." Plastic means "permanently distorted" if the forces are too large. Source: docbrown OCR 21C B pages.
- **Energy stored is GIVEN.** "energy stored in a stretched spring = ½ × spring constant × (extension)²" is on the OCR B data sheet, according to PMT-hosted copies of J259 question papers ("Use the data sheet").
- **PAG 2 (Forces) is the spring practical.** Its title is "Investigating the effect of forces on springs". Sources: PMT OCR B PAG 2 notes and flashcards, and the Cognito guide.
