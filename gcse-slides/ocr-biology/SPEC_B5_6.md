## Practical
No PAG is linked to B5.6 in any source found. Slides 17–18 are a **suggested activity** (orange pill): timed Benedict's test on glucose standards and simulated urine.
- Apparatus is drawn: water bath, five test tubes in the colour sequence, colour key, stop clock.
- The slides cover the method, IV, DV and control variables, safety, and a dilution worked example.
- Results: illustrative data in a table of 3 repeats and means, a calibration curve drawn to scale, interpolation for unknown Y, and sources of error with improvements.
- Data: means of 300, 190, 120, 75 and 48 s for 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 g per 100 cm³. The model is t = 120c^−0.66, and each mean is asserted to be within 3 s of it. Y = 95 s, which gives about 1.4 g per 100 cm³.

## Maths (52 asserted calcs)
- **Day glucose model (to scale):** 5.0 to 7.5 mmol/L.
- **Glucose in the blood:** 5 L × 5 mmol/L = 25 mmol, which is 4.5 g. QC: 40 mmol = 7.2 g.
- **Liver glycogen:** 100 g ÷ 4.5 g ≈ 22 (the 100 g is a typical textbook value, UNVERIFIED).
- **Feedback:** 8.2 − 5.0 = 3.2 mmol/L in 90 min, which is 0.036 per min.
- **HT hormone graphs (model curves to scale):**
  - glucose peaks at 28 min (7.7 mmol/L)
  - insulin peaks at 45 min (×9)
  - glucagon is lowest at 30 min and rises after 150 min
  - glucose is 4.4 mmol/L at 180 min
- **BMI:** 95 / 1.75² = 31.0. QC: 68 / 1.70² = 23.5. FQ: 102 / 1.8² = 31.5.
- **UK figures:** about 4.4 million diagnosed; about 90% type 2 and 8% type 1 (Diabetes UK, UNVERIFIED to the percent). This gives 3.96 million type 2, and a type 2 : type 1 ratio of about 11.
- **Glucose tolerance test:**
  - A: 4.8 → 7.8 mmol/L, a rise of 62.5%.
  - B: 8.0 → 15.0 mmol/L, a rise of 87.5%.
  - The difference between A and B at 2 h is 8.0 mmol/L.
- **Recaps, full question and homework:** 5.4 g, 2.4, 450, 100%, 6.2, 6.25%.
