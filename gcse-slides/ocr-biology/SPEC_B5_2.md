## Practical: ruler drop (slides 15–17)
- **Apparatus drawn:** a 30 cm ruler with 0 at the bottom; person A's hand at the top; person B's thumb and finger at 0; B's forearm on the bench.
- **Method:** 5 repeats with no distraction, then 5 while counting backwards.
- **Variables and safety:** included.
- **Errors and improvements:** included.

Data are illustrative and asserted in node:

| Condition | Trials / cm | Mean / cm | t / s |
|---|---|---|---|
