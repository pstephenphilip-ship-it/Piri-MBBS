## Graphs (drawn to scale and asserted in node)
- **Hormone levels, slide 9.** Smooth model curves are built from Gaussian bumps and repeated every 28 days, so day 0 joins day 28. Relative levels are in arbitrary units.
  - The pituitary panel (FSH, LH) and the ovary panel (oestrogen, progesterone) share the day axis with a lining-thickness strip.
  - Asserted: LH peaks on day 13.6; oestrogen peaks on day 12.3, which is before the LH surge; progesterone peaks on day 21; progesterone is low before ovulation; FSH is lower in the luteal phase.
- **Lining thickness, slides 6 and 9.** Piecewise model: 10 mm on day 0, 1.5 mm on day 5, 9 mm on day 14, 11 mm on days 21–27. These are typical textbook values (UNVERIFIED).
