## Practical: PAG B1, root tip squash (slides 12–13)
**Apparatus drawn** in four tiles:
1. A garlic clove rooting over water, with a scalpel and tile.
2. A boiling tube of HCl in a 60 °C water bath, with a thermometer.
3. Stain on a slide, a coverslip, and a thumb pressing through paper.
4. A light microscope.

**Method:** cut a 5 mm tip; HCl at 60 °C for about 5 min, then rinse; add toluidine blue (or acetic orcein) stain; squash; view at ×100 then ×400; count cells in each stage. The slides also cover variables, hazards, and sources of error with improvements.
