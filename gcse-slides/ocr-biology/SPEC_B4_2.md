## Teaching values (rounded; stated in the notes)
- Cell and organelle sizes:
  - cheek cell 60 µm; onion epidermis cell 250 µm;
  - mitochondrion 2 µm; bacterium 2 µm; chloroplast 5 µm;
  - ribosome 25 nm; virus 100 nm; atom 0.1 nm.
- Microscope performance:
  - light microscope: up to about ×2000, resolution about 200 nm;
  - electron microscope: up to about ×2 000 000, resolution about 0.2 nm (texts vary between 0.1 and 2 nm).
- History dates are from memory: Hooke 1665, Brown 1831, Benda 1898, Ruska & Knoll 1931, Palade cristae 1952–53 and ribosomes 1955.
