## Equations
- RECALL: Q = I × t (10.9); E = Q × V (10.6); V = I × R (10.13); P = E ÷ t (10.29); P = I × V (10.31); P = I² × R (10.32).
- GIVEN (Physics Equations List): E = I × V × t (10.27). **This differs from the task brief, which listed E = IVt as recall.** The spec says "Use the equation", not "Recall and use", and a search extract of the Pearson-based equation list includes it. The deck labels it GIVEN.
- From earlier topics: efficiency (Topic 3, recall); T = 1/f (Topic 4) is used once, in the 50 Hz worked example.

## Core Practical
- **CP 10.17 is the only Core Practical in Topic 10.** Edexcel does *not* have a separate "I–V characteristics of lamp, diode and resistor" core practical, unlike the AQA required practical.
- CP 10.17 covers (a) V, I and R for a resistor and a filament lamp, and (b) testing series and parallel circuits with resistors and filament lamps.
- Diodes, thermistors and LDRs come under 10.18 and 10.21 ("explain how the design and use of circuits can be used to explore…"). They are not part of the Core Practical, and the deck treats them on separate content slides.
- Revision sites give this practical different ordinal labels (PMT "CP 5a", Save My Exams "Core Practical 4"). The deck uses the spec number 10.17.
- Deck coverage: the circuit drawn with native symbols, method, variables, part (b), safety, a results table, graphs to scale, R = V/I, errors and improvements.
