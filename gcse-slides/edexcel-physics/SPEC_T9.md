## Equations
- RECALL: moment of a force = force × distance normal to the direction of the force (9.7P, physics only). The deck writes it as M = F × d.
- The principle of moments (9.8P) is a rule to recall. It is not an equation-sheet item.
- From earlier topics, all RECALL: F = m × a and W = m × g (Topic 2), E = F × d (Topic 8), efficiency (Topic 3).
- Topic 9 has no GIVEN (equation-sheet) equations.
- g = 10 N/kg is used throughout.

## Core Practical
- **None in Topic 9.** The 1PH0 Core Practicals are 2.19, 4.17, 5.9, 5.19P, 10.17, 14.3, 14.11 and 15.6. Cognito says "eight mandatory core practicals"; spec numbers 2.19 and 4.17 were confirmed by search.
- Slides 24–26 are a **suggested practical**: the principle of moments, using a metre rule on a knife-edge pivot. They cover apparatus drawn to scale, method, variables, a results table, a graph of clockwise against anticlockwise moment, errors, improvements and safety.
