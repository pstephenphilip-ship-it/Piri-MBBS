## Equations
- RECALL: average speed = distance ÷ time (2.6); a = (v − u)/t (2.8); F = m × a (2.15); W = m × g (2.16); p = m × v (2.24, H).
- GIVEN (Physics Equations List): v² − u² = 2 × a × x (2.9); F = (mv − mu)/t (2.26, H).
- Links used in the deck: KE = ½ × m × v² (Topic 3, recall) and work done E = F × d (Topic 8, recall), on slide 38.
- g = 10 m/s² = 10 N/kg throughout. Moon g = 1.6 N/kg (example only).
- The deck adds the new equations to the shared EQS table at run time, in build_T2.js. edx_template.js is unchanged.

## Core Practical
- **CP 2.19:** "Investigate the relationship between force, mass and acceleration by varying the masses added to trolleys". This is the only Core Practical in Topic 2. PMT calls it "CP 1 Newton's Second Law"; Save My Exams calls it "Core Practical: Investigating Force & Acceleration".
- One Save My Exams summary says the Topic 2 core practical is "force–extension for springs". This is wrong: that is CP 15.6 in Topic 15. We ignored it.
- Deck coverage (slides 25–29):
  - apparatus drawn: tilted runway on a block, trolley, card, two light gates, data logger, bench pulley, string, mass hanger
  - method for both versions: vary mass at fixed force, and vary force at constant total mass
  - light-gate calculation
  - variables
  - safety
  - results table and graph to scale (a against m, with the theoretical F/m curve dashed)
  - analysis (m × a ≈ F)
  - systematic and random errors, and improvements
  - recap
