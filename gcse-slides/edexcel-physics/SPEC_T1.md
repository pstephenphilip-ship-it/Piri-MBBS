## Content the brief asked for that is NOT a Topic 1 statement
These are labelled with green pills in the deck ("WORKING SCIENTIFICALLY" / "PRACTICAL SKILLS"). The codes follow the DfE GCSE physics subject content, which Edexcel reproduces in its Working Scientifically section and its maths skills appendix. The code letters are from memory (UNVERIFIED); the content is examinable on both papers.
- **Orders of magnitude and estimation:** Working Scientifically 4c, "use prefixes and powers of ten for orders of magnitude" (this exact phrase also turned up in search); maths skills 1d (estimates) and 2h (order of magnitude calculations). Slide 17.
- **Reading scales, resolution:** Working Scientifically 2f, making and recording measurements with a range of apparatus. Slide 19.
- **Means, anomalies, uncertainty:** Working Scientifically 3c, and maths skill 2b (arithmetic means). Slide 20.
- **Accuracy, precision, random and systematic error:** Working Scientifically 3g, evaluating data for accuracy, precision, repeatability and reproducibility, and finding sources of random and systematic error. Slide 21.
- **Derived units (N, J, W, Pa, C, V, Ω, Hz):** these are part of 1.1 through the specification's SI-units appendix, which lists derived units with special names (search extract of a school revision sheet based on it).
- **kWh to J:** the Topic 1 statements do not name the kWh. The deck uses it as a 1.3 conversion example (slide 13; full question (d)). The teacher can drop it.
