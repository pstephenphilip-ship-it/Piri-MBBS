## Equations
- RECALL: F = k × x; p = F / A (physics only). Earlier topics: W = m × g, ρ = m / V.
- GIVEN (Physics Equations List): E = ½ × k × x²; P = h × ρ × g (physics only).
- g = 10 N/kg is used throughout, as in Edexcel papers.

## Core Practical
15.6: investigate the extension and work done when applying forces to a spring. The deck covers method and apparatus, variables, safety, a results table, a graph drawn to scale with the limit of proportionality, k from the gradient, work done from the area, and errors.
