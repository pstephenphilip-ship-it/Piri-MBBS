## Equations
- RECALL: v = f × λ; v = x ÷ t (4.6).
- Also used: period T = 1 ÷ f (illustrative link; not a listed equation).

## Core Practical
- 4.17 (both courses): ripple tank (fluid) and a metal rod struck with a hammer, frequency from a phone app/oscilloscope, λ = 2 × rod length (solid).

## Tier and course markers
- **H:** 4.16P (medium-low). One search summary claimed ultrasound is Higher only; not supported elsewhere, ignored.
- **P:** 4.8P, 4.9P, 4.12P–4.16P. Combined (1SC0) classes skip those slides.

## Illustrative values
Speed of sound in air 340 m/s (330 also accepted); in water 1500 m/s; P waves 6 km/s and S waves 3.5 km/s in the crust (rounded).
