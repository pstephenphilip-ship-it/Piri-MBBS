- Ar values (Edexcel periodic table): H 1, C 12, O 16, Na 23, S 32, Cl 35.5, Ca 40, Cu 63.5.
- CP 3.6 model: 50.0 cm³ of 0.40 mol dm⁻³ HCl (0.020 mol H⁺); Ca(OH)₂ (Mr 74) in 0.10 g portions; end point at 0.74 g. pH before the end point = −log[H⁺ left]; after it, excess OH⁻ capped at a saturated Ca(OH)₂ solution (illustrative, rounded to 1 d.p.). Real results rise less sharply because the solid dissolves slowly.
- CP 3.17: 25 cm³ of 1.0 mol dm⁻³ H₂SO₄ → 0.025 mol CuSO₄·5H₂O (Mr 249.5) → 6.2 g theoretical; 4.1 g obtained → 66 % (link to Topic 5 percentage yield).
