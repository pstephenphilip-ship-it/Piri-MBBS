## Practical
- OCR's combined-science chemistry PAGs are C1 electrolysis, C2 distillation, C3 separation, C4 salts and C5 rates (see SPEC_C5_1.md).
- Cognito says Combined B learners do not need titration as a PAG. Titration is PAG 6 in J258 (separate) only.
- The titration procedure is still a C5.3 statement (C5.3.6), so slides 14–15 carry the label **SUGGESTED PRACTICAL**.
- **Slide 14** covers apparatus, method and safety:
  - **Apparatus drawn:** clamp stand, burette, tap and jet over a conical flask on a white tile. Two magnified burette readings show the bottom of the meniscus (0.20 and 22.65 cm³).
  - **Method:** rinsing, rough titration then dropwise near the end point, repeat until concordant within 0.10 cm³.
  - **Variables:** the titre is measured; pipette volume, indicator and swirling are controlled.
  - **Safety (CLEAPSS):** NaOH 0.1 mol/dm³ IRRITANT; HCl 0.1 mol/dm³ low hazard; phenolphthalein in ethanol HIGHLY FLAMMABLE; use a pipette filler; fill the burette below eye level.
- **Slide 15** covers results:
  - Results table: rough plus three runs, with initial and final readings and titres.
  - Mean titre 22.50 cm³.
  - A pH-against-volume curve plotted to scale. The curve is calculated in node from the chemistry, not sketched. The phenolphthalein range is shaded.
- **Slides 18–19** cover errors and quality of data:
  - parallax, % uncertainty of burette, pipette and measuring cylinder
  - random vs systematic error, anomalies
  - a dot plot of three students' data, drawn to scale

## Equations (atom and charge balance asserted in node)
- NaOH(aq) + HCl(aq) → NaCl(aq) + H₂O(l)
- H⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq) → H₂O(l)
- H₂SO₄ + 2NaOH → Na₂SO₄ + 2H₂O
- KOH + HNO₃ → KNO₃ + H₂O
- 2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂ (starter)
- Na₂CO₃ + 2HCl → 2NaCl + H₂O + CO₂ (notes)
- Two unbalanced test cases must throw.

## Maths (83 asserted calcs; data illustrative)
- Mr values computed from the formulae: NaOH 40, KOH 56, HCl 36.5, HNO₃ 63, H₂SO₄ 98, Na₂CO₃ 106, NaCl 58.5.
- **Concentration in g/dm³:**
  - 5.0 g in 250 cm³ = 20 g/dm³
  - 40 × 0.0250 = 1.0 g; 6.0 ÷ 30 = 0.20 dm³
  - 10/0.25 = 20/0.5 = 40/1 = 40 g/dm³
- **Concentration in mol/dm³ (HT):**
  - 4.0 g NaOH in 250 cm³ = 0.40 mol/dm³
  - 0.100 mol/dm³ HCl = 3.65 g/dm³; 9.8 g/dm³ H₂SO₄ = 0.10 mol/dm³
- **Standard solution:** 2.65 g Na₂CO₃ for 250 cm³ of 10.6 g/dm³ (= 0.100 mol/dm³).
- **Practical:**
  - titres 23.10, 22.45, 22.55, 22.50; mean 22.50
  - c(HCl) = 0.00250 ÷ 0.02250 = 0.111 mol/dm³ (4.06 g/dm³)
  - pH 13.0 at the start, 7 at 22.50 cm³, 1.52 at 40 cm³
- **1 : 2 example:** 0.125 mol/dm³ H₂SO₄.
- **Uncertainty:** 0.44%, 0.24% and 2.0%.
- **Evaluating data:** student means 22.50, 22.37 and 23.40; ranges 0.10, 1.10 and 0.10.
- **Full question:** mean titre 20.10; 0.0734 g HCl; NaOH 0.0804 mol/dm³.
