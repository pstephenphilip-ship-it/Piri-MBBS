## Practical: PAG C5 Measuring rates of reaction (Combined; J258 equivalent PAG 8)
**Slide 16: method (gas syringe, concentration)**
- Apparatus drawn: conical flask, bung and delivery tube, a horizontal 100 cm³ gas syringe in a clamp stand, a stopclock, Mg ribbon in the acid, and the bench.
- Variables:
  - IV: acid concentration (1.0 and 0.50 mol/dm³)
  - DV: volume of H₂ every 10 s
  - controls: 4.0 cm Mg (0.040 g), 50 cm³ acid, temperature
- Worked HT check that the acid is in excess.
- CLEAPSS-style safety:
  - HCl below 2 mol/dm³: LOW HAZARD
  - Mg: FLAMMABLE
  - H₂: EXTREMELY FLAMMABLE
  - wear eye protection

**Slide 17: results**
- A results table at 9 times for both concentrations, with an anomaly at 40 s (plunger stuck).
- A graph to scale of both curves.
- Mean rates over the first 20 s: 1.3 and 0.80 cm³/s.
- HT initial rates: 2.0 and 1.0 cm³/s.
- Conclusion, errors and improvements.

**Slide 18: disappearing cross (temperature)**
- Apparatus drawn: eye, flask, paper with X.
- A table at 20–50 °C with 3 repeats; the anomaly at 40 °C (36 s) is excluded.
- Means: 98, 52, 28, 15 s. 1000 ÷ t: 10.2, 19.2, 35.7, 66.7.
- A graph to scale with an exponential fit.
- Safety: SO₂ TOXIC (asthma, ventilate, do not heat above 50 °C).
- Errors and improvements.
